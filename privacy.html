Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SocialMind handles information when connecting to supported social platforms.

1. Overview

SocialMind is an AI-assisted desktop application for creating, reviewing and preparing social media content.

SocialMind is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information.

2. Social platform authentication

When a user chooses to connect a supported social media account, authentication is performed through the platform's official authorization process.

SocialMind does not request the user's social media password directly.

3. Information received from TikTok

Depending on the permissions explicitly granted by the user, SocialMind may receive limited account information from TikTok, such as an account identifier, basic profile information and authorization permissions.

4. Authentication tokens

Authorization tokens may be stored locally on the user's computer so that SocialMind can maintain the authorized connection.

These tokens are used only to perform actions requested or approved by the user through SocialMind.

5. Content

SocialMind may process locally generated or user-provided videos, captions, hashtags, scripts, images and related content in order to prepare social media posts.

6. Data sharing

SocialMind does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a social platform only when required to use an authorized integration or publish content requested by the user.

7. Local storage

SocialMind may store application data, generated media, publishing information and authentication information locally on the user's device.

8. Third-party services

When SocialMind connects to third-party services, those services process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Data retention

Locally stored application information remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or by SocialMind's account-disconnection or reset functions.

10. Account disconnection

Users may disconnect supported social accounts from SocialMind. Local authorization information associated with the connection may then be removed.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored authentication data. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SocialMind develops additional features or platform integrations.

13. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the project owner or official SocialMind communication channel.
