What is SocialMind?

SocialMind combines AI-assisted research, content generation, media creation and human review into a single workflow for creating short-form social content.

🔎 Research

Identify topics and content opportunities using external information sources.

✍️ Content

Prepare scripts, voiceovers, captions and social media copy.

🎬 Media

Generate visual assets, voiceovers, subtitles and vertical video.

✅ Human Review

Content can be reviewed, edited and explicitly approved before publishing.

Publishing workflow

SocialMind is designed around a review-first workflow.

1. Discover

Select a topic or content idea.

2. Create

Generate text, visual assets, voice and video.

3. Review

Review and edit content before it is approved.

4. Publish

Approved content can be prepared for supported social platforms.
